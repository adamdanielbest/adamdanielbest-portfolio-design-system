<p>Rapid experimentation across the early trials was generating insight that was not being shared effectively across the team. Different workstreams were learning things independently, and the gap between what users were telling us and what fed into roadmap decisions was longer than it needed to be.</p>

<p>I ran stakeholder interviews across the Customer Experience (CX) team, commercial and our network partner VMO2 to map what was already known before we ran new research. Alongside this I standardised our insight library, introducing a consistent approach to tagging and categorising findings so that desk research could be done quickly without re-running the same discovery work. This gave product leads a reliable starting point when making trade-offs in real time.</p>
