<p>Launching a 0→1 vertical in a regulated utility space required a tiered approach to risk. Rather than designing in isolation and launching at scale, we built through progressive exposure: discovery, internal staff trials, a small member cohort, a controlled regional launch, and finally a national commercial launch.</p>

<p>The small cohort phase was the most valuable. We ran field visits alongside the installs, observing and interviewing members during the actual engineer visit, capturing the emotional reality of the experience in a way no remote research could replicate. Each phase gave us real contact rate and satisfaction data before the next cohort went live, so service logic failures and UI gaps were caught and resolved before scaling. The product that launched nationally was substantially different from the one we started with.</p>
