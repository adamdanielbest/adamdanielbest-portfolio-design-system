<p>Rather than designing a complete product and launching at scale, we built in stages: internal staff trials, a small member cohort, a controlled regional launch, and then the national commercial launch.</p>

<p>The small cohort phase was the most useful. We ran field visits alongside the actual installations, observing and talking to members during the engineer visit. That gave us a picture of the emotional reality of the experience that remote research could not replicate. Each phase produced real contact rate and satisfaction data before the next cohort went live, so problems were caught and fixed before scaling. The product that launched nationally was substantially different from the one we started with.</p>
