<p>My main contribution was designing the logic of the acquisition and onboarding journey. Three concurrent third-party integrations shaped what the experience could and could not do, and each could alter the user's path in ways we could not predict in advance.</p>

<p>The address check, using Loqate to look up the Unique Property Reference Number (UPRN) for each property, was the entry point. It determined whether the property was a flat or a house and fed into what type of installation would be needed. The installation slot could not be confirmed until a regulatory switch request was registered via SureSwitch, which is governed by Ofcom's One Touch Switching (OTS) regulation. Each of these introduced a degree of uncertainty into the journey that could not simply be designed away.</p>

<p>The key decision was to be honest with members about that uncertainty rather than hiding it behind false progress states. I worked with engineering to make the interface reflect each stage accurately, including the ambiguous ones, so that members understood what was happening even when we were waiting on external systems.</p>
