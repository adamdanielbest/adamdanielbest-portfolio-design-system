<p>giffgaff is a mobile virtual network operator known for its community-led model and challenger brand positioning. In 2023 the business decided to enter the Full Fibre broadband market — a move that had no existing playbook internally and sat outside its core product infrastructure.</p>

<p>The challenge wasn't just building a sign-up flow. Broadband is a fundamentally different product to SIM-only mobile: it involves installation appointments, engineer visits, activation windows, router delivery, and a long pre-go-live state that MVNO customers had never experienced. Every state had to be communicated clearly, or the support burden would be immediate.</p>

<p>I joined as the sole product designer on the Full Fibre squad, working across a cross-functional team of PMs, engineers, a service designer, and an external delivery partner.</p>
