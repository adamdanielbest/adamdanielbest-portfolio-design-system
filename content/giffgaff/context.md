<p>giffgaff is a Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) running on O2's network, known for its member-led model and low-overhead approach to customer service. In 2023 the business entered the Full Fibre broadband market, a move with no existing playbook and infrastructure that sat entirely outside its core SIM-only product.</p>

<p>Broadband is a fundamentally different product. It involves installation appointments, engineer visits, activation windows, router delivery and a long lead time between order and go-live that MVNO customers had never encountered. Every stage in that journey had to be communicated clearly, or the support load would arrive before the product was even live.</p>

<p>I joined as the sole product designer on the Full Fibre squad, working in a cross-functional team with product managers, engineers, a service designer and an external delivery partner.</p>
