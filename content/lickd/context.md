<p>Lickd is a music licensing platform that lets YouTubers, streamers and video creators license tracks from major labels legally, and without the copyright claims and demonetisation that come with unlicensed music. The platform had two plan types: pay-per-licence for commercial tracks, and subscription for stock music.</p>

<p>The licensing model was necessarily complex. Per-track licence terms varied by artist and label, and what a creator could do with a track depended on their channel size, their platform and the specific agreement in place. The obvious fix, hiding that complexity, was not available to us. Creators needed to understand what they were buying, or the claims would come anyway.</p>

<p>I joined as the sole designer across product and brand, working with a product manager and an engineering team of around six.</p>
