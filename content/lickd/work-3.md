<p>Alongside the core checkout work, I ran a research programme with the Innovation Hub, a recruited community of active Lickd creators. Monthly sessions gave us a direct line to the people using the platform regularly, and surfaced longer-term friction that would not show up in analytics alone.</p>

<p>The main findings shaped a prioritised roadmap: immediate fixes to the checkout experience, and a set of longer-term improvements to how Lickd communicated with creators between purchases. Email and in-app messaging were doing a lot of work to fill gaps in the product, but the messages were generic. Creators with large channels needed different guidance from those just starting out.</p>

<p>I documented the findings and worked with the product manager to translate them into a roadmap that the engineering team could sequence alongside other priorities. Not everything made it into the immediate release, but the research gave us a shared basis for deciding what to build first and why.</p>
