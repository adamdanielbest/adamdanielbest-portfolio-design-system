<p>HubbleHQ is an office search and flexible workspace marketplace. Businesses use it to find and book office space, from monthly flex desks to long-term private offices. The platform connects workspace seekers with hosts, and a large part of that process involves physical viewings before any commitment is made.</p>

<p>The viewings flow had a structural problem that no amount of design could remove: hosts controlled their own calendars, and many were slow to respond or unavailable at the times they had listed. A third of viewing requests were being declined by hosts, and there was no way for the product to prevent that. We could only design around it.</p>

<p>I joined as the lead product designer, working with a product manager, engineering team and, for part of the project, a second designer focused on the host-side experience.</p>
