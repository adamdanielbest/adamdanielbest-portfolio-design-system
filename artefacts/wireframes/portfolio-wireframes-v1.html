1dCase study page template — applies to all three
← back to work · Company · Role · 2019–2020
HOOK — one strong statement or image, sets the stakes
Context — the business situation, why this mattered, constraints
Role — what you owned, team, scope, timeframe
Approach — process, key decisions, artifacts — this is where depth lives
Outcome — metric, impact, quote — the payoff
↔ prev / next case study · back to index
Same five-beat rhythm every time (hook → context → role → approach → outcome) gives scanners a reliable place to jump in and deep-readers a consistent structure. Heading style + spacing scale repeats across all four studies so the site itself reads as one system.
